Mountain Climbing

How to start Mountain Climbing.

Here are some starter tips for Mountain Climbing

  • Start off with beginner trails to get experience on hiking first.
  • Build your fitness by practicing walking uphill, improving endurance, and carrying lightweight equipment.
  • Make sure to get the right equipment when ready like comfortable footwear, weather appropriate clothing, backpack, and any other reliable navigation tools.
  • Take time to learn the basics in Climbing. For example, take a beginner course or hire a guide to take with you on the climb to help out navigating through the Mountain.
  • Lastly, make sure to climb with a group/team, as it's the safest way to make sure you aren't lost or in danger when there is a unexpected situation occurs.
